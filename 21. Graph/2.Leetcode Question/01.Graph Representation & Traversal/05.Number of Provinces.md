## 📌 Problem

There are `n` cities. Some of them are connected, while some are not.

If city `a` is directly connected with city `b`, and city `b` is directly connected with city `c`, then city `a` is indirectly connected with city `c`.

A **province** is a group of directly or indirectly connected cities.

You are given an `n x n` matrix `isConnected` where:

- `isConnected[i][j] = 1` → city `i` and city `j` are directly connected.
- `isConnected[i][j] = 0` → city `i` and city `j` are not directly connected.

Return the **total number of provinces**.

# Example

Suppose:

```text
isConnected =

[
 [1, 1, 0],
 [1, 1, 0],
 [0, 0, 1]
]
```

We have **3 cities**:

```text
City 0
City 1
City 2
```

The matrix tells us whether two cities are directly connected.

|        | City 0 | City 1 | City 2 |
| ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ |
| City 0 | 1      | 1      | 0      |
| City 1 | 1      | 1      | 0      |
| City 2 | 0      | 0      | 1      |

So:

```text
0 ↔ 1

2
```

City `0` and `1` are connected.

City `2` is completely separate.

Therefore:
 
```text
Province 1 → {0, 1}
Province 2 → {2}

Answer = 2
```
